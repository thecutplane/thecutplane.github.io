CUTPLANE: how we check our facts

Every number, date and name said in our videos is listed here with the sources it was checked against. Research is done with AI-assisted tools; each claim must be backed by an official report or two independent sources, and the full list is reviewed and approved by the channel editor before a video is published. Narration and animation are produced with AI tools.

EP01: Air Transat 236, the wrong part

Reviewed and approved by the channel editor on 2026-11-23.

137 facts checked. 137 verified.
Main source: Accident Investigation Final Report 22/ACCID/2001 (GPIAA), Air Transat A330-243 C-GITS (full 103-page report)

Research notes

All claims were checked against the complete GPIAA final report (22/ACCID/2001, 103 pages); the FAA, Flight Safety Foundation and other summaries are listed as supporting sources. Where sources disagree we use the official report: second engine flameout about 65 nm from Lajes (other sources say 78 or 85), 67.5 flight hours since the engine change (one source says about 60), and a trim-tank delay of the imbalance message of about 15 minutes (one summary says 25). The report itself gives the first touchdown as 1,030 ft (p.8) and 1,050 ft (p.11), so we say about 1,000 ft. The exact wording of the FUEL IMBALANCE checklist caution appears in the report only as a figure, so we paraphrase it. 'About a ton every five minutes' is our arithmetic from the maximum leak rate of about 13 tonnes per hour. The report records time pressure and concern about the missing service bulletin, but no mechanic objecting to the aircraft's release; the screwdriver marks on the tubes are a hypothesis, not a confirmed fact.
